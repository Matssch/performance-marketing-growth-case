# Growth challenge

## Business context

MoveMove is a digital mobility platform focused on entrepreneurs and businesses. The customer journey was largely digital, which meant Marketing had a direct impact on both the volume and efficiency of customer acquisition. The challenge was not simply to generate more traffic, there was already meaningful demand coming into the website.

The bigger opportunity was to improve how efficiently that demand turned into customers. At the same time, we needed to keep growing acquisition without allowing CPA to increase beyond a sustainable level.

## The commercial challenge

The main question was:

**How can we acquire more customers while improving the efficiency of the complete acquisition funnel?**

That meant looking at more than media performance. The full journey mattered:

**Traffic → Landing experience → Conversion → Onboarding → Customer**

If paid media generated more traffic but the website did not convert, additional spend became expensive. If conversion improved but traffic quality dropped, the result could still be weak. If customers converted but the onboarding journey created friction, value was still being lost later in the process.

The growth challenge therefore required acquisition, conversion and customer journey optimisation to work together.

## Starting point

At the start of the optimisation period, the main indicators showed clear room for improvement:

* Website traffic: approximately 40.000 monthly visits
* Website conversion rate: approximately 1.2%
* CPA: approximately €100
* Significant reliance on Paid Search
* Limited Paid Social activity
* Opportunities to improve website and onboarding journeys

The objective was to improve performance without simply solving the problem by spending more.

## Growth target

The working CPA target was approximately **€65**, with a maximum acceptable level of around **€75**. That created a clear commercial constraint. Growth was valuable only if customer acquisition could remain efficient enough to scale. The goal was therefore to improve three things at the same time:

### 1. Increase qualified traffic

Grow relevant demand through the right mix of acquisition channels.

### 2. Improve website conversion

Turn a higher percentage of existing traffic into customers.

### 3. Improve the customer journey

Reduce unnecessary friction between acquisition, conversion and onboarding.

## Why conversion became a key growth lever

One of the most important conclusions was that acquisition performance could not be managed only inside the advertising platforms. If website conversion improves, the economics of every acquisition channel improve with it.For example:

+ At a 1.2% conversion rate, 10,000 visits generate approximately 140 conversions.

+ At a 2.4% conversion rate, the same 10,000 visits generate approximately 280 conversions.

The traffic volume has not changed, the commercial output has doubled. This made CRO one of the strongest growth levers available.

## Growth approach

Instead of treating channels, website and CRM as separate marketing activities, I approached them as parts of the same acquisition system. The working model became:

**Acquire better traffic → Improve conversion → Improve onboarding → Measure customer acquisition → Reinvest based on performance**

This created a stronger connection between marketing activity and customer growth.

## The key principle

The main lesson behind the case is:

**Performance marketing does not stop at the click.**

The quality of the traffic, the landing experience, the conversion journey and what happens after conversion all determine whether acquisition can scale efficiently.
