# Customer Journey

## Why I looked beyond the conversion

A completed form or order is an important conversion.

It is not the complete customer journey.

For the mobility company, the commercial journey continued after the website conversion through onboarding and activation.

That meant I looked at growth across a wider journey:

**Demand → Website → Conversion → Onboarding → Active Customer**

If Marketing improves the first half of that journey while customers struggle in the second half, part of the acquisition value is still lost.

This is why I saw the website, CRM and onboarding experience as connected parts of the same growth system.
