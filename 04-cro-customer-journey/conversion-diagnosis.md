# Conversion Diagnosis

## Why CRO became a priority

At the start of the optimisation period, the website converted at approximately **1.2%**.

Traffic was already meaningful, at around **40,000 visits per month**.

That created an important question:

**Do we need more traffic, or are we leaving too much value on the table from the traffic we already have?**

The answer was both.

Acquisition still needed to grow, but improving conversion had the potential to change the economics of every acquisition channel at the same time.

That made CRO one of the strongest growth levers available.
