# Commercial Impact

## What changed

The performance marketing work was focused on improving the complete acquisition system rather than optimising individual channels in isolation.

The main areas of work were:

- Paid Search optimisation
- Introduction of Paid Social
- Customer segmentation
- Website and landing-page optimisation
- Conversion rate optimisation
- CRM and onboarding improvements
- Funnel measurement
- Budget optimisation

The objective was simple:

**Acquire more customers while improving the efficiency of the acquisition model.**

---

## Starting point

At the beginning of the optimisation period:

- Website traffic was approximately **40,000 monthly visits**
- Website conversion rate was approximately **1.2%**
- Paid acquisition CPA was approximately **€100**
- Paid Search represented the majority of digital acquisition
- Paid Social was still a limited part of the acquisition mix

This created two main growth opportunities:

1. Improve the value generated from existing traffic
2. Build additional acquisition capacity without losing control of CPA

---

## Results

Across the growth period:

| Metric | Starting point | Improved state |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monthly website traffic | ~40K | ~54K |
| Website conversion rate | ~1.2% | ~2.6–3% |
| Paid acquisition CPA | ~€100 | ~€70 |
| Peak new customers | — | ~1,700 / month |

These results did not come from one campaign or one isolated optimisation.

The improvement came from working across several parts of the acquisition system at the same time.

---

## 1. More qualified traffic

Traffic increased from approximately **40K to 54K monthly visits**.

The objective was not simply to increase website volume.

The focus was on bringing more relevant demand into the funnel through:

- Paid Search
- Paid Social
- Affiliate marketing
- Organic growth
- Brand activity

Paid Social was introduced as an additional acquisition channel, reducing dependence on Search and creating another route into the funnel.

---

## 2. Stronger conversion

Website conversion increased from approximately **1.2% to 2.6–3%**.

This was one of the biggest growth levers.

The important effect was not only a better website KPI.

Higher conversion improved the economics of the acquisition channels feeding traffic into the website.

The relationship was:

**More qualified traffic × stronger conversion = more customers without proportional media growth**

This is why I treated CRO as part of performance marketing rather than as a separate website discipline.

---

## 3. Lower acquisition cost

CPA moved from approximately **€100 toward €70**.

The target was around **€62.50**, with approximately **€70** as the maximum sustainable operating level.
